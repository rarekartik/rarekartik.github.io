A little door, Anshu

If you feel like reading something more personal, tap below and enter the little key. Take your time. This part is written from my heart.

Anshu,

I don't know if I say this enough, but I'm genuinely grateful that you came into my life. You have been part of so many ordinary days that I now remember fondly. Your time, your care and your little gestures have meant a lot to me.

I still remember our first WhatsApp video call on 22 August 2025, at 23:09. Who knew that one call would become so many late-night conversations, silly talks and memories that I would keep close? I still smile when I think about those days.

Thank you for bringing me lunch, for getting medicines for me when I wasn't well, for checking on me, and for all the things you did without making a big deal out of them. I noticed every bit of that care. I hope you know how much it mattered.

And your birthday gift to me — that wallet and the wish you made, that it would always stay full of money. I remember it with so much affection. It wasn't just a wallet; it was your thoughtfulness, wrapped in a simple little gift. I will always treasure that.

I love you, Anshu. I don't say that to ask you for anything or to make you feel responsible for my feelings. I just want to be honest about the affection and gratitude I carry. You are important to me, and I'm glad I got to know you.

I hope life is kind to you. I hope your family stays happy and healthy, your dreams find their way to you, and you always have reasons to laugh. Please remember to take care of yourself too. You deserve the same tenderness you so naturally give to others.

Whatever changes with time, I will always be thankful that our paths crossed. I'm glad you exist, Anshu. Truly.

Someone who will always wish the best for you.
With love — Kartik
