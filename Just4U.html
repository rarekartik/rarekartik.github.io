A personal note · private corner

A little door, Anshu

If you feel like reading something more personal, tap below and enter the little key. Take your time. This part is written from my heart.

Anshu,

I have started writing this a few times in my head, and every time I feel that words are going to fall short. Not because there is nothing to say, but because there is so much I remember and so much I feel grateful for.

I still remember our first WhatsApp video call — 22 August 2025, at 11:09 at night. I don't think either of us knew how many conversations, laughs and little moments would follow. Those late-night talks, sometimes stretching till two or three, became part of my routine. I remember how naturally we could keep talking, even when there was probably nothing important left to discuss.

And then there are the things you might not even remember doing. Bringing me lunch. Getting medicines for me when I was unwell. Checking on me. Being thoughtful in ways that you may consider normal. I noticed, Anshu. I really did. Those gestures made me feel cared for, and I don't want to take them for granted.

I remember my birthday wallet too. The ₹20 note and the one-rupee coin you placed inside, along with your wish that my wallet would always stay full. I have kept that wish close. It wasn't the price of the gift that mattered. It was the thought behind it, and the fact that it came from you. That little note and coin mean more to me than their value could ever explain.

I won't pretend that every part of our story has been easy, or that I have always known how to express myself perfectly. There have been moments when I felt too much, when I missed the old conversations, and when I didn't know where to place all the affection I carry. But I don't want this letter to become a list of expectations or a request for anything. I simply wanted you to know what your presence has meant to me.

I love you, Anshu. In a way that holds all these memories, all this gratitude, the fondness, the friendship, and the softness I feel when I think of you. I am not writing this to ask you to answer it in any particular way. I just don't want to hide the truth of what you mean to me behind a casual “Happy Princess Day”.

I hope you get the life you quietly wish for. I hope your mother stays well, your family stays happy, your hard days become easier, and you get every opportunity you work for. I hope you keep laughing loudly, being a little silly, changing your mind, growing, and becoming whoever you want to become. And whenever life feels heavy, I hope you remember that you deserve care too — not only to give it to everyone else.

Thank you for entering my life, for the memories already made, and for the kindness you have shown me. Whatever shape life gives our friendship in the years ahead, I will always be glad that I got to know you.

Someone who will always wish the best for you.
With love — Kartik
One little thing I hope you remember

That wallet is still special to me. The note, the coin, and your wish are safe with me. Some gifts become memories; this one became a promise I carry quietly.
